# Norge360 translation-review release checklist

English is the canonical source language for the MVP. A language must not be shown as fully translated until a professional or local editor has approved the current English source copy for each scope below:

- onboarding
- plan
- task descriptions
- legal notices and regulatory disclaimers

For every language and scope, record the reviewer’s name or organization, review date, source-copy version, and any terminology decisions in the team’s approved localization workflow. Legal and procedural content must also be checked against the task’s cited official Norwegian source; a translation review is not an immigration, tax, or legal eligibility determination.

Before release, verify that right-to-left layouts work for Arabic, Persian, Urdu, and Dari; that Dynamic Type and VoiceOver remain usable; that official terms such as `Skattekort` and `D-nummer` retain a clear plain-language explanation; and that task titles, descriptions, source labels, and disclaimers have all been reviewed together.

Until this record exists, the app intentionally displays the English source copy for the selected language. This prevents development placeholders or incomplete translations from being presented as verified guidance.
